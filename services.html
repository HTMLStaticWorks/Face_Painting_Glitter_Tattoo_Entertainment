How Events Work

Everything you need to know before booking SparkleMagic for your event.

How Events Are Conducted

We arrive early, set up a clean self-contained station, and run a smooth queue system so guests are served efficiently with minimal wait time.

What Happens At The Event

Guests choose from curated design boards, artists complete each design with premium products, and we adapt flow in real time for children, adults, and mixed groups.

When Events Are Available

Bookings are available on weekdays and weekends, including school holidays and evening events, subject to date/time availability in your area.

How To Book Or Enquire

  1. Send your event date, location, and estimated guest count via our contact page.
  2. Receive service/package recommendations and a clear quote.
  3. Confirm your slot to secure your booking.

For larger festivals, school fairs, or corporate family days, mention expected footfall so we can propose the right artist team size.
